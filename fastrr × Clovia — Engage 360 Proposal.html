About Engage 360

The order is where the relationship starts, not where it ends

Engage 360 is fastrr's customer engagement platform. It brings every Clovia customer conversation, from order confirmation to the next purchase, onto the channels customers already read: WhatsApp, Instagram, email, SMS and RCS, with AI calling for COD verification. A dedicated Growth Manager runs the journeys, tests and reporting, so the platform keeps working long after launch.

Fewer failed COD deliveries

An AI call verifies each COD order before it ships, and the RTO Suite flags risky orders early.

Questions answered where customers ask

Instagram and WhatsApp chatbots and the on-site AI Assist handle size, fit and order questions instantly.

More repeat orders

Post-delivery journeys, new drops and restocks on WhatsApp bring customers back for the next purchase.

What's included

Everything in Engage 360

One platform, six building blocks. Each one targets a point where D2C brands typically lose customers or margin.

WhatsApp — marketing, utility & chat

Launches, restocks and cart recovery on marketing; confirmations and shipping updates on utility; customer-initiated sessions at no per-message cost.

Instagram & WhatsApp chatbots

Both included in the platform fee. They answer product, size and order questions in the DMs where Clovia's audience already is.

AI Calling for COD confirmation

An AI call verifies a COD order before it ships, so fewer parcels come back as failed deliveries.

Email, SMS & RCS

Email for long-form content and style guides, SMS for fast alerts, and RCS for richer, tappable messages where supported.

AI Assist Chatbot on the site

An on-site shopping assistant for fit and size questions, with an add-to-cart card built into the chat.

A dedicated Growth Manager

Included in the platform fee. A named person runs journeys, tests and reporting, so Engage doesn't become a dashboard nobody opens.
